Privacy Policy

Data processing in Join

Join is a kanban-based project management app created as part of the Developer Akademie. This privacy policy explains how data is handled before signing up or logging in, and while using the app as a registered user or guest. Before login, only the data required to display public pages such as this Privacy Policy is processed. After login, Join may use account information, contacts, tasks, priorities, due dates and board data to provide the task management features of the application.

Your data and rights

You decide which information you enter into Join. Please do not add sensitive personal information to tasks, contacts or comments. The data you provide is used only to display and manage your board, assign tasks, show deadlines and keep the project state available to you and your team. Join does not sell personal data. If data is no longer needed, it should be corrected or deleted. For questions about stored data, access, correction or deletion, please contact the Join project team.
